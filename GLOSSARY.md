# njil.dev

Nathan's personal site: work history, projects, and a blog with an email newsletter.

## Language

### Blog

**Blog post**:
A piece of writing published to `/blog`, authored in the CMS.
_Avoid_: Blog (for a single post), article

### Newsletter

**Newsletter**:
The email list for Blog posts, and everything that manages it: who is on it and what they're sent.
_Avoid_: Mailing list, subscription service

**Subscriber**:
An email address that has joined the Newsletter. A Subscriber is either active (receives email) or inactive (kept on record, receives nothing).
_Avoid_: Member, user, contact

**Welcome email**:
The single email a Subscriber receives when they join or rejoin the Newsletter.

**Announcement**:
The email sent to every active Subscriber when a Blog post is published.
_Avoid_: New-blog email, newsletter blast, notification
