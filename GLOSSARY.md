# njil.dev

Nathan's personal site: work history, projects, and a blog with an email newsletter.

## Language

### Career

**Job**:
A position Nathan has held at a company, listed on `/work` and written up on its own page at `/work/<job>`.
_Avoid_: Work, experience, role

**Project**:
Something Nathan built outside of a Job, shown on `/projects`.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, side project

**Contribution graph**:
Nathan's GitHub contributions for the last year, one count per day, shown on the homepage.
_Avoid_: Activity graph, commit graph, heatmap

### Blog

**Blog post**:
A piece of writing published to `/blog`, authored in the CMS.
_Avoid_: Blog (for a single post), article

### Newsletter

**Newsletter**:
The email list for Blog posts, and everything that manages it: who is on it and what they're sent.
_Avoid_: Mailing list, subscription service

**Subscriber**:
An email address that has joined the Newsletter. A Subscriber is either active (receives email) or inactive (kept on record, receives nothing).
_Avoid_: Member, user, contact

**Welcome email**:
The single email a Subscriber receives when they join or rejoin the Newsletter.

**Announcement**:
The email sent to every active Subscriber when a Blog post is published.
_Avoid_: New-blog email, newsletter blast, notification

### Contact

**Contact message**:
A note a visitor sends Nathan from `/contact`, with an optional name, the visitor's email address and the message itself. Nathan replies to it by email.
_Avoid_: Message (on its own), enquiry, submission, contact
